Other tokens
Focus2 px amber ring, 2 px offset, on every interactive part. Same colour as the playhead: it means where you are.
TouchMinimum 44 × 44 px. Hit areas may be larger than the drawing. Pointer-only density: 26 to 36 px on desktop.
LinesSeam 1 px in seam colour with a 1 px light line beneath. Cables 3.5 px, audio 4.5 px.
GrainHorizontal repeating line, 1 px in 3, 1.6 % white and 2.5 % black. On every panel.
MotionPlayhead, recording lamp and rendering pulse only. Reduced motion stops the pulses. Key travel is 2 px, no easing curves.
Z orderPanel, plate, content, playhead, toast, scrim, sheet.
