Synth tab

A console you can hold in one hand.

A modular synth and a DAW for psytrance and cinematic textures, inside Creation Studio. Local synthesis and AI generation sit side by side. The workflow is familiar to anyone who knows a channel rack, a playlist, a piano roll and a mixer. The look is ours: steel panels, masking-tape labels written in marker, amber for signal, red for recording and money.

Hard rules · how they are met
TouchEvery target is at least 44 × 44 px. Step cells, ports, faders and toggles are 44 px on touch; a dense 26 to 36 px mode appears only for pointer devices. Note rows in the roll stay 44 px through Fold to scale.
Not by colourPorts differ by shape, cables by line style, lit states by a lamp and a raised or pressed key, meters by a CLIP word as well as the red lamp, mute and solo by lamp and label.
ContrastBody text 9.0:1 on slate, dim text 5.9:1 and 5.0:1 on plates, ink on tape 10:1. Red and blue text use lighter tally-text and blue-text tokens.
MotionOnly three things move: the playhead, the recording lamp and a rendering pulse. Under reduced motion the pulses stop and state stays readable as text and a bar.
No side scrollThe page never scrolls sideways. Only the timeline and roll canvases pan, and each shows a scroll thumb and an edge cue.
Drag or tapEvery drag has a tap alternative: tap a knob for number entry with − and +, tap a note or handle for an inspector with steppers, tap port A then port B.
